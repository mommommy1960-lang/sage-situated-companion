# Sage tablet demonstration October 24 2026

Mya P. Brown reported the following during a supervised walkthrough on her Android tablet:

- Live camera preview displayed her surroundings.
- Location permission was granted and coordinates were displayed. Exact coordinates were not requested or published.
- After pausing a competing voice call and trying again, speech recognition placed her spoken words in the question box.

These are founder-reported device observations, not an independent certification or a broad accuracy benchmark. Voice transcription is input, not a generated answer. Audible spoken replies remain unconfirmed. Camera preview does not interpret scenes.

## Follow-up changes

Ordinary tab switches now retain the active session, approvals, question, answer and pending model request. Explicit Stop still clears consent, releases devices and discards pending output. Actual navigation/closing releases device streams. Browser or OS background suspension remains outside this application's control. This change supersedes the prior policy that paused the session whenever the page was hidden.

Added Test Sage's voice independent of the model, installed-voice selection, speech event/error status, a speech-start diagnostic and optional automatic playback of new answers while visible. Retained utterance references and explicit volume/rate/language improve the browser playback path. These changes require audible validation on the tablet; adding a control is not proof of sound.
