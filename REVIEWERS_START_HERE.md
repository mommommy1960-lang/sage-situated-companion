# Reviewers Start Here

SAGE Situated Companion is an early architecture for a persistent, situated companion with contextual continuity, memory, intervention logic, and future hardware integration.

## Review focus

Evaluate sensor-data minimization, consent, retention and deletion, local processing, capability boundaries, intervention safeguards, and safe hardware disconnects.

## Evidence boundary

SAGE is an architecture and prototype runtime. It is not a production medical, safety, or always-correct companion.

Read [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS), and the main [README](README.md).


## Phase 1 hardware feasibility

Read the [October 14 non-confidential feasibility brief](docs/PHASE1_FEASIBILITY_BRIEF_2026-10-14.md) for requirements, open inputs and proposed tests. The preliminary intake was prepared and sent; Minewing's assessment remains pending. No physical prototype, safety certification or manufacturing authorization is established.
