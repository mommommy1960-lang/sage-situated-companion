THE PATH TO A PHYSICAL COMPANION

Built for demonstration. Ready for the next engineering step.

Available in this interface
Permission controls, camera preview, browser voice input, location display, model text responses and spoken playback.

Next to prove
Scene understanding, reliable background operation, source-grounded answers, persistent consented memory, bicycle and wearable hardware.

Your control remains central
Stop closes device streams and clears local location. Switching tabs keeps your session and approvals. Closing or navigating away releases device streams; the browser may suspend background activity. This is not a navigation or hazard-detection system.
